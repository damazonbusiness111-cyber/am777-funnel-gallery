Automation System

Private schools need cleaner enrollment management — not more spreadsheets. This is an online, cloud-ready system that organizes your entire admission workflow from first parent inquiry to confirmed enrollment, with nothing falling through the cracks.

Enrollment Inquiry Capture

Every parent inquiry gets logged the moment it comes in — no more missed messages or scattered notes across your team.

  • Parent name, contact number, email
  • Student name, grade level, school year
  • Inquiry source (Facebook, referral, walk-in, website)
  • Staff notes and initial assessment

Student Admission Tracker

Move each applicant through a clear, stage-based pipeline so your admissions team always knows where every student stands.

  • New inquiry → For follow-up
  • Requirements pending → Assessment / interview scheduled
  • Enrolled or not continued
  • Full history log per student record

Requirements & Document Checklist

Track submission status for every required document per applicant — always know what's missing without digging through folders.

  • Birth certificate, report card, good moral cert
  • ID photo and medical records
  • Payment / reservation fee status
  • Per-student checklist updated in real time

Parent Follow-Up Workflow

Built-in reminders and message templates keep your team following up consistently — without manually chasing parents every day.

  • Automated follow-up reminder triggers
  • Ready-to-send message templates per stage
  • Enrollment deadline reminders
  • Hot lead tagging and parent concern notes

05Questions, Answered

Before you message us, read this.

Q.

What is this service all about?

+
AM777 sets up a complete lead-capture system on top of the Facebook Page you already have — a landing page, a 24/7 Messenger chatbot that auto-replies to inquiries, an inquiry tracker, automated follow-up reminders, and optional content posting. Every customer message gets a fast, organized response instead of getting lost.
Q.

Do I need a website already?

+
No. The landing page we build works on its own and connects directly to your Facebook Page and Messenger inbox. Most clients don't have a website before working with us — that's normal.
Q.

How does the 24/7 chatbot work?

+
When someone messages your Page, the chatbot replies instantly — even outside business hours. It answers common questions, collects the details you need, and flags the conversation so you can pick it up quickly. It feels personal, not robotic.
Q.

How long does setup take?

+
Starter and Growth setups are typically completed within 3–7 business days. The Full Local Lead System, with a month of content, usually takes 1–2 weeks since it includes more planning and asset creation.
Q.

Is there a monthly fee?

+
The packages shown are one-time setup fees. Ongoing content posting, monitoring, or optimization after the first month is available separately and quoted in advance — no surprise charges.
Q.

What if I'm not sure which package fits?

+
That's what the free evaluation is for. Answer four quick questions and we'll recommend a starting point — no pressure to upgrade until you're ready.
Q.

Is this a full ERP or school management system?

+
No — and that's by design. This is a focused enrollment and inquiry workflow system, not a full school ERP. It handles the front-end admission process: capturing inquiries, tracking applicants, managing document checklists, and keeping your team on top of follow-ups. It's built to be fast to set up and practical to use without a dedicated IT team.
Q.

Can this work for a small private school?

+
Yes. This system is specifically designed for small to mid-size private schools that don't have a large admin team. Whether you're handling 30 applicants a year or 300, the workflow scales with you. Setup is scoped to your school's actual size and processes — we quote based on what you need, not a one-size-fits-all package.
